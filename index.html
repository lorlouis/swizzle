Swizzle

Turn Google Form responses into a file for Notion, with the right column names and order.

How does it work?
  1. Add your survey responses. Open the Google Sheet with your form responses, then choose File → Download → Comma-separated values (.csv). Drop the downloaded file in box 1.
  2. Send each answer to a Notion column. Each row reads left to right: a question from the Google Form → the Notion column it goes to. Start with all the survey questions, then rename, reorder or remove them. The order of the rows is the order of the columns in Notion.
  3. When the question to use depends on another answer (for example the title comes from a different question for a conference or a publication), choose “Depends on another answer…” on the left and add one rule per answer. Use the a→b button to replace text on the way (fix spelling, shorten answers…).
  4. Already using IFS(…) formulas in Google Sheets? Open Google Sheets formulas at the bottom of step 2 to import them, or to turn your setup back into formulas to paste into Google Sheets.
  5. Save your setup (in Saved setups at the top) so next time you only need to drop in the new responses file.
  6. Download the file for Notion, then in Notion use Import → CSV (new page) or, in an existing database, ••• → Merge with CSV.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere: your files stay on your computer.

        Import formulas from Google Sheets

        1. Add the responses file in step 1 (the column letters in the formulas are matched to its questions).
        2. In the Google Sheet that has your formulas, choose View → Show → Formulas (or press Ctrl+`).
        3. Select the row with the column names and the first row of formulas below it (e.g. rows 1 and 2), copy, and paste here.

          Export as Google Sheets formulas

          In an empty sheet of the same Google Sheets file, click cell A1 and paste: row 1 gets the Notion column names, row 2 the formulas. Then select row 2 down to the last response and press Ctrl+D (⌘D on Mac) to fill the formulas down. Or copy one formula at a time:
